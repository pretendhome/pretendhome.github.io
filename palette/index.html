A free app for your computer. Give it one folder. It lists what is there without opening a file, reads only what you allow, and builds a document from the facts you accept. Every line leads back to the sentence it came from.

§ 02The rules it keepsEach one can be checked in the app

Most AI apps tell you what they will do for you. This one is easier to describe by what it will not do without you.

  • 01

    It never goes looking.

    It works on the folders you hand it, one at a time. It does not search the rest of the computer on its own.

  • 02

    It reads nothing until you say so.

    Listing a folder opens no file. Reading starts when you press Grant, and every file it read is listed afterwards.

  • 03

    Nothing in the document is made up.

    Today the document is the facts you accepted, in a fixed layout. If a fact is not in your notes, it is not in the document. Written summaries come next, held to the same rule: every sentence leads back to a source.

  • 04

    Nothing it keeps is hidden.

    On Home, what you asked for is kept and listed on screen in the same turn, and every version of the document is kept. In a matter, your words wait on a card until you keep them.

  • 05

    It does not need a model to work.

    The sample runs with no AI model installed and no key. The app says so on screen.

  • 06

    It keeps a list of what it connects to.

    The app keeps a list of the connections it has made. On a fresh install, after the whole sample, that list reads “none yet”.

§ 04What it is forAnd what it is not

Good for

  • A review, brief or summary sheet assembled from scattered notes
  • Finding out where a number in a document came from
  • Seeing what is in a folder before anything is allowed to read it
  • Work where a wrong sentence is expensive

Not for

  • Searching the web
  • Your inbox or your calendar
  • Booking, buying or doing errands
  • Free writing: emails, essays, posts

Today It reads plain text: .txt, .md, .csv, .json and .ics. PDFs and Office files are listed and skipped, and it tells you so. It is an early build, and it is honest about where it stops.
