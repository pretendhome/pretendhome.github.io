§ 01ScopeBuilt for

An event and a museum look different, but they run on the same things: an order, a payment, a ticket, a gate. This canvas models those once and lets everything else be configuration.

  • 01

    Congresses and conferences

    Registration fees with early-bird and late-fee rules, conditional registration forms, badges from print fields, check-in with anti-passback, live statistics during the day.

  • 02

    Museums and permanent venues

    Year-round ticketing with no artificial event: day tickets and rolling passes, visitor profiling at the desk and online, end-of-shift cash reconciliation.

  • 03

    Festivals and fairs

    Multiple locations and gates, operators self-assigned by QR, real-time occupancy per hall, time-window rules for reduced tickets.

  • 04

    Online plus box office

    One catalogue, two checkouts. Order provenance is frozen at creation, so revenue is analysed by channel: gross, tax, fees, net.

  • 05

    Agencies running many clients

    One user, many tenants, switched on the fly, each with its own licence, modules and roles.

  • 06

    Connected back offices

    Rules on order and ticket events push signed webhooks to the ERP, with throttling and a delivery ledger for anything that failed.

§ 03Supported today15 modules · 80 features

Everything this canvas already does.

The inventory below is what ships before we change a line. It is a floor, not a ceiling: the features your company needs are defined in the audit and built on top.

  1. M-01

    Platform and governance

    Multi-tenant foundations, licensing and access control.

    • Every domain record scoped by tenant
    • Per-tenant licence, seats and activatable modules
    • Role-based access: 25 resources × 10 actions, with a permission matrix editor
    • Audit log on every mutation
    • Notes, typed labels (single and bulk), saved table views
    • Hot tenant switching for users working across organisations
  2. M-02

    Events

    The event as a first-class object, from draft to archive.

    • Lifecycle: draft → published → postponed / cancelled / archived
    • In-person, online and hybrid formats
    • Event profile: branding, contacts, accessibility, catering
    • Early-bird and late-fee rules with windows, channels, stacking and preview
    • Attendees: batch processing from orders, labels, CSV export
    • Live page: occupancy per location, last-hour flow
    • Public event landing by slug
  3. M-03

    Venues and locations

    Sell on a place, not only on a date.

    • Permanent-venue mode: no artificial event required
    • Locations with capacity, groups and checkpoints
    • Location maps with typed pins for gates, stands and services
    • Operators self-assign to a checkpoint by scanning a QR
    • Dedicated venue workspace
  4. M-04

    Catalogue

    Products, add-ons, stock and tax.

    • Products with SKU, price, stock and image variants
    • Reusable add-ons and add-on groups with min/max and price override
    • Tax rates per tenant, inclusive or exclusive, with overrides per product and offer
    • Pessimistic stock locking: no overselling
    • Deep clone of a checkout configuration
  5. M-05

    Checkout

    A configurable point of sale, online or in store.

    • Branding, texts, terms and language per checkout
    • Offers with ordering, price override and single-quantity purchase
    • Stripe payment account and offline bank transfer
    • Public link and QR
    • Per-checkout statistics
  6. M-06

    Form engine

    Structured data collection, versioned and reusable.

    • Immutable published versions: historic data stays readable
    • Drag-and-drop builder with live preview
    • Conditional visibility, also evaluated server-side
    • Mappings to contacts, orders, ticket print lines and profiling
    • One renderer shared by admin, storefront and POS
  7. M-07

    Orders

    Three channels, one authoritative quote.

    • Created from storefront, back office or POS
    • Event or venue provenance frozen at creation
    • Prices always recalculated server-side
    • State machine with requires-attention on balance mismatch
    • Cancel with used-ticket protection and stock return
    • Mark as paid, single and bulk; CSV export with dynamic columns
  8. M-08

    Payments

    Stripe as the source of truth, fees included.

    • Stripe Connect per tenant with OAuth onboarding
    • Signed, de-duplicated webhooks
    • Provider fee and net retrieved per transaction
    • Four collection paths: online, free, bank transfer, POS
    • Summary by gross, tax, fee and net
  9. M-09

    Ticketing

    Typed tickets, issued once.

    • Idempotent issuance on processed orders
    • Fixed or rolling validity (for example three days from first entry)
    • Day and time rules, allowed locations, maximum uses
    • Bundles, anti-passback and optional auto check-in
    • Locally generated QR; A4 and 80 mm thermal PDF
    • One email per order; signed print URLs
  10. M-10

    Check-in

    A complete decision at every gate.

    • Checks expiry, remaining uses, validity, time rules, location and passback
    • Every denied attempt stored with a typed reason
    • Checkpoint derived from the operator, never from the payload
    • Real-time occupancy and cross-location statistics
    • Bundles recorded as one aggregated entry
  11. M-11

    POS

    The box office, with a cash drawer that adds up.

    • Terminals with server-verified PIN
    • Shift opening and closing with reconciliation
    • Expected versus declared, mismatch flagged
    • Deposits and withdrawals as recorded movements
    • Preferred products per terminal; thermal printing
    • Unreconciled transactions always visible
  12. M-12

    Contacts and CRM

    A CRM fed by the sale itself.

    • Dynamic attributes with PII classification
    • 360° view: orders, tickets, check-ins, notes
    • Duplicate detection with merge preview
    • Automatic creation from orders, enriching without overwriting
    • CSV export
  13. M-13

    Profiling

    Know your visitors without writing code.

    • Tenant-defined typed dimensions
    • Fed directly by form mappings
    • Statistics by checkout, location, POS, checkpoint and period
    • Sensitive dimensions excluded from reports by default
  14. M-14

    Notifications and automations

    An event bus you can build on.

    • Transactional outbox with idempotent handlers
    • Rules: conditions → email, signed webhook or in-app
    • Liquid templates per tenant
    • Throttling windows against bursts
    • Delivery ledger with retry and manual resend
  15. M-15

    Tenant administration

    Everything an operator needs to run their own house.

    • Members, invitations and role editor
    • Logo, language, currency and timezone
    • Payment accounts and tax rates
    • Scheduled jobs with manual run
    • Audit log viewer

§ 04Agents on this canvasOrdered data in · reliable work out

Structured enough for agents to be trusted.

Agents fail on ambiguity. A canvas removes it: typed data, explicit rules and a record of everything that happened, connected to the rest of your company.

  • Every state change is an event

    Orders, payments, tickets, check-ins and shift closings are published on a transactional event bus. An agent subscribes to what matters and acts on it.

  • Rules decide, agents explain

    The gate says no with a typed reason. An agent can turn that into an answer for the visitor, or a pattern for the operations manager.

  • Reporting without the spreadsheet

    Gross, tax, fees and net are already separated by channel and location, ready to be read, compared and summarised.
