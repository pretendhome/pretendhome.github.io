Questions.

What is Mission Canvas?

Software built around how your business already works, running on your own computers. We audit how the work moves, build the application from a working starting point for your industry, and hand it over to you.

Who is it for?

Small and mid-sized businesses whose work runs on documents, records and rules. That includes construction, education, logistics, front desk and voice, events and venues, with healthcare and office work in progress.

How is it different from ChatGPT or other AI chat tools?

Chat tools answer questions in someone else's cloud. Mission Canvas builds an application around your workflow and runs it on your computers. The AI works inside it, with your rules and records, and an outside model is used only for the work you allow.

What happens in the audit?

We sit with your team and map how the work really moves: the tools, the files, the handoffs. We measure where the time goes. You get a process map and a list of where the hours go. No contract, nothing to prepare.

How long does it take?

Four to six weeks from the audit to handover, depending on what the audit finds.
