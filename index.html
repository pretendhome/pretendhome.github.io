§ 04Your business stays yours

Your business stays yours.

Your application runs locally on hardware you control. Your company data stays under your control, and you choose which models you use.

Mission Canvas checks every request before the AI reasons — sensitive work stays on your machine, everything else goes to the right model, and every decision is logged.

We keep your personal data on your machine, and let you work with the outside world through a protected handoff: we filter what leaves for personal information.

Your data sits behind a socket firewall. If it cannot be enforced, the app does not start.

  1. 01 Scan

    Index what already exists.

    It runs on your own machines. It reads the files, exports and records you already have.

  2. 02 Extract

    Recognise what the business is made of.

    Customers, orders, documents, people, assets, and processes, identified wherever they are written down, in whatever format they were left.

  3. 03 Connect

    Connect it into one model.

    Entities are linked into one model of how your company actually works. That model is what gives agents meaning, not just text to search.

  4. 04 Serve

    Serve it to agents.

    Agents operate on ordered, pre-processed knowledge within your perimeter. Frontier models are called only when needed, and only with the minimum context.

  • Runs on your hardware

    A workstation or a server you already own. No data migration, no new cloud account.

  • Nothing leaves without a record

    The heavy lifting happens locally. What reaches an external model is filtered first, and every decision is logged.

  • Local models first

    The application runs on models on your own machine. An outside model can be added for the work you choose to send out, and only that work leaves.

  • The architecture is ours, and patented

    Mission Canvas classifies a request before a model runs, and nothing downstream can reopen that decision. Four provisional applications with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, across three independent families. We did not patent the classifier — we patented the constraint that makes classifying first mean anything.
