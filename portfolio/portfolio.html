Mical Neill · Founder, Mission Canvas

Make the work clear enough to use, check and hand over.

I build software and the tools people need to use it well. My work connects language, evaluation and the everyday decisions a team has to make.

My background spans teaching, knowledge systems and multilingual voice design. The question stays the same: what does someone need to understand before they can act?

Why I started

After leaving AWS, walking into Mission Rossi in San Francisco helped me see how much small businesses need useful AI. I started Palette to help people get real work done while retaining control of their data.

That visit is part of my founder story, not a customer endorsement.

The company

Mission Canvas adapts software around a business workflow and runs it on the business’s own computers. Palette is our free local app. They are related, but they are different offers.

For firms supporting many customers, the starting point is one recurring job: understand the inputs, agree the output and decide how the customer will check it.
